How-to-join

To join our cooking club, you need to use the code kchhghs on Google Classroom and fill out the google form . This code will also give you full access to our online group page. Once you are logged in, you can see all of our upcoming announcements, recipes, and important schedules. It is also the main place where we share details about how to pay the fee and how to get ready for our first cooking project together!
